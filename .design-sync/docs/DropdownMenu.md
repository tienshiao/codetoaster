Action menu on a trigger. `DropdownMenuContent` holds `DropdownMenuItem`s, with `DropdownMenuLabel` and `DropdownMenuSeparator` for structure, `DropdownMenuCheckboxItem`/`DropdownMenuRadioGroup` for stateful options, `DropdownMenuShortcut` for key hints, and `DropdownMenuSub` for nesting. Items are actions, not navigation targets.
