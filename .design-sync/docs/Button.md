The single action primitive. Variants (`default`, `destructive`, `outline`, `secondary`, `ghost`, `link`) and sizes (`default`, `sm`, `lg`, `icon`) come from `buttonVariants`, a class-variance-authority recipe you can also call directly to style a non-button element as a button. Use `asChild` to render a link or menu trigger with button styling. Icon-only buttons take `size="icon"` and need an accessible label.
